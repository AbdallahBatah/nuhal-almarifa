Official license

Licensed by Dubai's Knowledge and Human Development Authority

The Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA) is the government body that regulates private education in Dubai, including training centers. Our license means our programs, facilities and practices operate within its regulatory framework and standards.

Quality cycle

Six stages every program goes through

01

Needs analysis

Interviews and surveys to identify the real performance gap.

02

Outcome design

Measurable learning objectives and content built on real cases.

03

Pre-assessment

Measuring participants' level before starting to set depth and examples.

04

Interactive delivery

Exercises, workshops and simulations led by specialist trainers.

05

Post-assessment

Measuring what participants learned against the pre-assessment.

06

Impact report

A report for the entity with results, recommendations and a follow-up plan.

Measuring impact

Four levels we use to measure success

An evaluation framework inspired by the Kirkpatrick model, linking what happens in the room to what changes at work.

1. Reaction

Participant satisfaction with content, trainer and environment, through an end-of-day survey.

2. Learning

The difference between pre- and post-assessment results in the targeted knowledge and skills.

3. Application

How far participants apply what they learned at work in the weeks after the program.

4. Impact

The program's effect on the entity's performance indicators agreed during diagnosis.

FAQ

About licensing and certificates

Is Nuhal Almarifa licensed by KHDA?

Yes. Nuhal Almarifa Training & Development L.L.C is licensed by Dubai's Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA).

What is the Knowledge and Human Development Authority?

It is the government authority that regulates private education in Dubai, including training centers, and sets the frameworks and standards licensed institutions follow.

How can I verify a training center's license in Dubai?

Licensed education providers can be checked through the official channels of the Knowledge and Human Development Authority at khda.gov.ae.

What does the certificate of completion include?

The certificate shows the participant's name, program title, training hours and dates, and is awarded to everyone who meets the attendance and assessment requirements.

Does your organization have a specific training need?

We design the program with the content, duration and location that suit your team, in Dubai, any other emirate or abroad.
